# DreamSolve

A sleep-first app. Set your bedtime; one hour before, DreamSolve guides you through your personalized wind-down routine. That's it — every feature serves those two things.

## Status

🌙 Pre-development. See [RESEARCH.md](RESEARCH.md) for the competitive landscape, MVP scope, screen map, and tech-stack decisions.

## The idea

- You set a **bedtime**.
- Exactly **60 minutes before**, the app flips into routine mode.
- It walks you through your wind-down steps one at a time, full-screen, with a countdown.
- At T-0 it says "Lights out. Goodnight." and gets out of the way.

No sleep tracking. No content library. No social. Just bedtime.

## Stack (planned)

Vite + React + TypeScript · Tailwind · Zustand · `vite-plugin-pwa` · IndexedDB (`idb-keyval`) · Framer Motion.
